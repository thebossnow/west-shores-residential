Privacy policy

Last updated August 17, 2026. This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have counsel review it before you publish, especially for CCPA and any SMS or call tracking you add.

What we collect

When you contact us by phone, email, or the form on this site, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the work you are asking about.

Standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visited. If analytics or advertising tools are added to this site, list them here.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and schedule an evaluation
  • To prepare estimates and perform contracted work
  • To communicate about a job in progress, including with lenders, escrow, or engineers when you have asked us to
  • To keep records required for licensing, permitting, insurance, and tax purposes

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate, such as email, form handling, and scheduling tools, and with parties directly involved in your project when you have asked us to. We also disclose information where the law requires it.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Update this section to match whatever messaging platform you actually use.

Retention

We keep project records for as long as licensing, warranty, and tax requirements call for, then dispose of them securely.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Contact us at anderson.co.billing@gmail.com or PHONE_NUMBER.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
