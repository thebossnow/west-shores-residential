Services

From the highway to the last hookup.

Three service lines, one crew: moving the home, setting it level on a proper foundation, and connecting the utilities that make it livable.

Moving & transport

Getting the home from A to B, legally and in one piece.

Transport & hauling

Permitted road moves for mobile and manufactured homes, with route planning and oversize-load coordination handled.

Site prep & placement

Preparing the destination site and positioning the home once it arrives, ready for foundation work.

Teardown & blocking

Disconnecting utilities and blocking the home for transport, done in the right order so nothing gets damaged.

Foundation & setup

Structural work most contractors skip.

Leveling

Shimming and re-supporting the home so the frame carries load evenly. Sticking doors, cracked drywall corners, and sloping floors usually trace back here.

Foundation & pier repair

Replacing failed or undersized piers, correcting spacing, and rebuilding supports to spec with rated materials.

Pads & supports

New pads set on prepared ground, with support spacing matched to the home's frame and setup requirements.

Skirting & tie-downs

Anchoring, tie-down systems, and skirting installation that seals the perimeter and stands up to sun and wind.

Utility connections

Hooking the home up once it's set.

Water & sewer

Tying the home into site water and sewer or septic, with proper slope and connections.

Gas & electrical

Gas line and electrical service connections, coordinated with the utility and inspector.

Under-home plumbing

Leak detection, repipes, drain repairs, and crawlspace line work before moisture reaches the subfloor.

Vapor barrier & moisture

Barrier installation and drainage corrections for damp crawlspaces, musty odors, and rusting supports.

Also available

Evaluations and certification support.

Structural evaluations, photo documentation, and 433A and HUD certification assistance for financing, escrow, and sale.
