Plain English

What a 433A actually is.

In California, Form HCD 433A is the document recorded with the county once a manufactured home has been installed on a permanent foundation system. Recording it ties the home to the land, which is what most lenders and title companies are looking for before they will treat it as real property.

An engineer signs the certification. Our part is the contractor side: getting under the home, documenting the existing foundation system, and performing the corrections the engineer or lender calls out so the certification can be issued.

We provide contractor structural evaluations and corrective work. We are not a licensed home inspection service, and we do not issue the engineer's certification ourselves.
